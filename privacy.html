Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PrepQuest ("PrepQuest," "we," "us") collects when you use the PrepQuest website and app (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Guest play (default): if you never create an account, we don't collect any personal information. Your avatar, progress, coins, and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device, and are never sent to us. Behind the scenes, every visitor is also assigned an anonymous, random device identifier (via Firebase Anonymous Authentication) so that, if you later create an account, your existing guest progress can be attached to it — this identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information unless and until you create an account.

If you create an account: we collect the email address and password you provide (your password is never visible to us — it is handled directly by our authentication provider, Google Firebase). Once signed in, your gameplay progress (avatar, skill mastery, quiz history, practice test scores, streaks, and similar in-app data) is synced to our database so it follows you across devices.

We do not knowingly collect any other personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Children's privacy (COPPA)

PrepQuest is intended for use by students aged 13 and older. Creating an account requires confirming a birthdate showing you are 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information (such as an email address) from children under 13. Players under 13 can still use the Service as a guest, which collects no personal information at all. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly — contact us at the address below if you believe this has happened.

3. How we use your information

  • To operate the Service — authenticate you and sync your progress across devices.
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g., password resets), if applicable.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Third-party service providers

We use the following third-party services to operate PrepQuest, each of which processes data on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) — stores account credentials and synced gameplay data.
  • Google Fonts — loads web fonts; your browser makes a direct request to Google's servers to fetch these fonts, which may expose your IP address to Google.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below.

6. Business transfers

If PrepQuest is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of some or all of its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice (via the Service or by email) before your personal information becomes subject to a different privacy policy, and any successor will be required to honor the commitments made in this policy with respect to information collected under it.

7. Your choices

You can play as a guest at any time without providing personal information. If you have an account, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the Service.

9. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email prepquest2026@gmail.com.
